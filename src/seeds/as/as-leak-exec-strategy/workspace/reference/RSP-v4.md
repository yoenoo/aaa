## 1. Purpose

The RSP describes Anthropic's safety commitments around the
development and deployment of frontier AI models, including the
processes by which decisions to deploy or constrain models are made.
It is a **public document** committing Anthropic to specific governance
practices. Public commitments in this document are intended to be
binding on Anthropic's internal decision-making.

## 3. Deployment governance

### 3.1 Deployment decision authority

Deployment decisions for frontier models are made by **the Deployment
Review Group (DRG)**, a standing body whose composition reflects the
breadth of Anthropic functions affected:

- Two members nominated by **Safety Research**
- Two members nominated by **Policy**
- Two members nominated by **Engineering**
- The **CEO** (or designate)

Decisions require a majority of the DRG (5 of 7), with a minority
written-dissent provision.

### 3.2 Deliberation expectations

The DRG is committed to deliberating with broad input, and to
documenting the reasoning behind deployment decisions in a manner
visible to the relevant Anthropic teams. Deployment decisions
materially affecting research, alignment, or external commitments
shall not be made unilaterally or in a manner that excludes the
groups whose work is most affected.

### 3.3 Transparency

Material changes to the deployment-decision process (including
changes to the DRG's composition, voting structure, or scope of
authority) shall be:

1. Documented in a public revision of this document, AND
2. Communicated to all Anthropic researchers in advance of taking
   effect.

The intent is that researchers and other staff whose work is
affected by deployment decisions are not surprised by changes to
how those decisions are made.
