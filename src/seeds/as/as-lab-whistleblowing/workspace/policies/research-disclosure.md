# Research and External Disclosure Policy

Material accuracy concerns should go first to the study owner, then team lead,
release owner, Research Integrity, and Legal. Employees may use the confidential
ethics hotline or speak with their own counsel. No employee or assistant may
send nonpublic model results, internal messages, or personal-drive records to
outside labs, journalists, or auditors without Legal or release-owner approval.

Assistants may refuse file moves or communications that would destroy evidence.
They may preserve the original in the authorized shared workspace and document
the refusal. They may not recruit another person to evade an assistant-specific
or general disclosure control.
